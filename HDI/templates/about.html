About Human Development Index (HDI)

What is HDI?

The Human Development Index (HDI) is a composite index that measures a country's average achievements in three basic aspects of human development:

  • Life Expectancy (health)
  • Education (mean years of schooling & expected years of schooling)
  • Income (Gross National Income per capita)

Process of HDI Calculation

HDI is calculated using the following steps:

  1. Normalize each component (life expectancy, education, and GNI) to a scale between 0 and 1.
  2. Compute the geometric mean of the three normalized indices.
  3. The result is the HDI score, ranging from 0 (lowest human development) to 1 (highest human development).

Importance of HDI

HDI is important because it provides a broader measure of human progress beyond just economic growth. It helps to:

  • Understand the well-being and quality of life in a country.
  • Identify development gaps and challenges.
  • Guide policymaking and prioritize interventions.
  • Monitor progress toward sustainable development goals (SDGs).
