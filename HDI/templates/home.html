🌍 Human Development Index (HDI)

The Human Development Index (HDI) is a composite measure that evaluates a country’s development based on life expectancy, education, and income. It provides a broader understanding of human well-being compared to economic growth alone.

HDI helps policymakers and researchers assess progress, identify gaps, and design strategies for sustainable development. Our system uses machine learning to enhance HDI predictions, providing deeper insights into social and economic progress.
