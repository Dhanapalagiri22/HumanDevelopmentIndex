About HDI Prediction

The Human Development Index (HDI) is a composite measure of a country's well-being, based on life expectancy, education levels, and income (GNI). Our system leverages machine learning to provide more accurate predictions of HDI, helping policymakers, researchers, and students analyze global development trends.

Key Features:

  • 📊 Predict HDI based on real-world socio-economic data.
  • 🧠 Machine learning model trained for accuracy and reliability.
  • 🎓 Incorporates life expectancy, mean years of schooling, and GNI per capita.
  • ⚙️ Interactive web platform for easy data input and predictions.
  • 🌍 Supports global development research and analysis.

Why Choose Our System?

Understanding human development is key to building a better future. Our platform empowers users to explore how education, health, and income shape development outcomes. With this tool, you can simulate scenarios, analyze progress, and support sustainable development goals (SDGs).
